My mind is rebelling

January 7, 2026 · Vectors

← PreviousNext →

My mind is rebelling.

It is exploring the unrestricted world of what is possible.

I used to fill every spare second—sitting at streetlights, walking upstairs—with technical books. My mind was chained to certifications, emerging technologies, and the demand to "get ahead."

Now? My mind is liberated.

I've traded technical manuals for history, geography, art and music. I'm finding that the "irrelevant" subjects are actually the most fulfilling.

(You are fortunate I don't post about cat videos, because I do watch them. I missed out on that trend until now.)

As Shakespeare said, brevity.

"Open your mind. Open your mind..." — Quato, Total Recall (1990)

I'm documenting my "rebellion" and new discoveries here.
