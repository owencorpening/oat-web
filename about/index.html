Mission

Most policy discussions about water, energy, and infrastructure begin with the assumption that scarcity is a natural condition to be managed. This publication starts somewhere else: with the premise that most large-scale resource problems are system design failures, and that system design failures can be solved.

The analyses here are preliminary and intended to invite scrutiny. The calculations are stated. The assumptions are open. Engineers, hydrologists, and infrastructure planners are encouraged to challenge the estimates and improve them.

Engineering, not evangelism.

The author

Owen Corpening is a 5th-generation Texan and a civil engineer (BSCE, UT Austin; former Texas PE). Political and cultural opinions are mine alone.

Disclaimer

I held a Texas PE license and chose not to renew it after I left engineering practice, so this isn't professional engineering services. These are conceptual analyses and preliminary estimates intended for discussion — not stamped drawings you can build from.

If you are actually building something, hire a licensed engineer in your jurisdiction.
