Privacy policy

Search Grid & Recency Filter for YouTube™ · Effective November 22, 2026

This extension collects no personal information and sends nothing anywhere.

What it stores

Two settings you choose in its popup: the card width and the maximum video age in days. They are saved with Chrome's own settings storage (chrome.storage.sync). If you have Chrome sync turned on, Chrome copies them to your other computers through your Google account, the same way it syncs your other Chrome settings. The extension's author never receives them.

What it reads

It runs only on youtube.com. To apply the recency filter it reads the date text shown on each video card, such as "3 days ago", in your browser. That text is used only to decide whether to hide the card. It is not saved or sent anywhere.

What it doesn't do

  • No analytics, tracking, ads, or cookies.
  • No network requests of its own and no remote code.
  • No reading of your browsing history, account, or anything outside youtube.com.
  • Nothing is sold or shared with anyone.

Permissions

Storage, to save the two settings. Access to youtube.com, to change how YouTube lists videos.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the extension does, with a new effective date.
