OAT stats

A personal reporting tool by Owen Corpening

OAT stats reads the Chrome Web Store statistics for Search Grid, my free Chrome extension, and charts them on a private dashboard next to my other publishing numbers.

What it does

  • Once a week it signs in to Google with read-only access to the Chrome Web Store and downloads the extension's daily totals: installs, uninstalls, active users, store-listing page views and impressions, broken down by country and by link source.
  • It saves those totals as spreadsheet files and rebuilds the dashboard from them.

It can't change anything in the Chrome Web Store, and it reads nothing else from the Google account.

Who it's for

Only me. It runs on my own computer, signed in to my own Chrome Web Store developer account. It isn't offered to anyone else, and nobody else signs in to it.
