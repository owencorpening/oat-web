Privacy policy

OAT stats · Effective November 26, 2026

OAT stats reads aggregate statistics about my own Chrome extension. It keeps them to itself and shares them with no one.

What it accesses

One permission, which Google shows as "See your Chrome Web Store items and their statistics" (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/chromewebstore.readonly). It is read-only. OAT stats uses it to download daily totals for one extension: installs, uninstalls, active users, store-listing page views and impressions, by country and by link source (UTM source and medium).

These are totals that Google has already aggregated. They contain no names, email addresses or other information about any individual who installed or viewed the extension. OAT stats doesn't ask for or read the account's name, email address, contacts, files or anything else.

How it's used

Only to chart the extension's adoption on my private dashboard. It isn't used for advertising, isn't sold, and isn't used to train AI models.

Where it's stored

  • The sign-in token is kept in the operating system's encrypted keyring on my computer. It is never written to a file or sent anywhere except to Google.
  • The downloaded totals are saved as CSV files on my computer and backed up to a private GitHub repository.

Sharing

None. Nothing from Google's APIs is shared with or transferred to anyone.

OAT stats' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. After that, OAT stats can read nothing more. To ask that saved data be deleted, use the contact link below.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated first, with a new effective date.
