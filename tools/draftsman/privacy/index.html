Privacy policy

Draftsman · Effective November 27, 2026

Draftsman runs on your computer and sends nothing to me. I don't collect, receive or store any of your information.

What Draftsman accesses

  • Your articles. It reads and edits the markdown files you point it at, on your computer.
  • Google Drive, only if you sign in. One permission, which Google shows as "See, edit, create, and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app" (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file). Draftsman uses it to create a new Google Sheet for each table you turn into a figure, write that table's rows into it, and share it so anyone with the link can view it, which lets readers of your post open it from the table image. It can't see or open any other file in your Drive, and it doesn't ask for or read your name, email address or contacts.
  • Image sources you turn on. When you search for an image, your search words go from your computer straight to that source (for example Pixabay, Openverse or Wikimedia Commons), under that source's own terms. Your own API key for a source goes only to that source.

How it's used

Only to do what you ask: lay out your article and, if you choose, make its table sheets. Nothing is used for advertising, sold, or used to train AI models. Draftsman has no analytics and no telemetry.

Where it's stored

  • Your Google sign-in and any API keys are kept in your operating system's keychain, or in environment variables you set. Draftsman never writes them to a file and sends them only to Google or the image source they belong to.
  • Images, credits and provenance records are saved beside your article. A search cache, undo history and batch plans are kept in Draftsman's cache folder on your computer (~/.cache/draftsman on Linux and macOS).
  • Table sheets live in your own Google Drive, and you own them.

Sharing

I receive nothing, so I share nothing. Each table sheet Draftsman makes is viewable by anyone who has its link, because that's what lets your readers open it. Your AI app asks before each table is turned into a sheet, unless you've turned on automatic changes for tables yourself. Nothing else from Google's APIs is shared with or transferred to anyone.

Draftsman's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your AI app

Draftsman's results go to the AI app you run it in, which handles them under its own privacy policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

  • Remove Draftsman's Google access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections. After that it can't reach your Drive.
  • Delete any table sheet in Google Drive, like any other file.
  • Delete the cache folder, or uninstall Draftsman, to remove everything else it kept on your computer.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated first, with a new effective date.
